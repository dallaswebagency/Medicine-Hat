Who Will Kleenway Services Send To Clean My Home?

Our Kleen Teem members are police cleared, dependable, trustworthy and trained to always be professional - for example, our members will never eat, drink, smoke or use any of your electrical appliances such as radios, microwaves or televisions while in your home.

We always try to clean your home using the same cleaning technician each time, unless due to sickness or an emergency, we are unable to do so. We want you to feel confident in our team members and they are eager to deliver a custom cleaning for your home as efficiently as possible.
